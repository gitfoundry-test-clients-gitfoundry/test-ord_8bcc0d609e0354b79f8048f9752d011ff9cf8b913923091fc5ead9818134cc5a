Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you get to a broken boiler?

We aim to reach Stoke-on-Trent homes within 24 hours of your enquiry, and sooner where we have gaps in the diary.

Should I repair or replace my boiler?

We diagnose first, then give you a straight answer. If a repair is sound and economical, we'll say so. If it isn't, we'll tell you why.

Do you work with landlords?

Yes. Landlords across North Staffordshire use us for servicing, repairs and bathroom work, with access arrangements organised directly with tenants.

How long does a bathroom installation take?

Most standard bathroom fits are completed within one to two weeks, depending on the specification. We confirm the schedule before starting.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the enquiry form on this page with a few details about the job. We'll come back to you with next steps and a quote.

Which areas do you cover?

Stoke-on-Trent and the surrounding North Staffordshire towns and villages. If you're just outside, send an enquiry and we'll confirm.
